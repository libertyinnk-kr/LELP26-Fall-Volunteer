📌 Program Overview

Program Overview
01

First year & Operating period

From 2021 to today (2026), LELP's continuous journey — every semester, growing together with students and volunteers.

02

What is LELP?

An online tutoring program that pairs North Korean students with volunteer tutors 1:1 for 2 hours a week over 12 weeks to practice English conversation.

03

Purpose

LELP helps North Korean individuals build confidence through English and access more opportunities. With ample learning opportunities, we support them to become agents of change.

What Is LELP?

01

A biannual English conversation program

The LiNK English Language Program (LELP) connects North Korean students with global volunteers for 1:1 English sessions held online, complemented by in-person activities.

02

2 hours a week, for 12 weeks

By meeting two hours a week for 12 weeks, volunteers help students improve their speaking skills, build confidence, and prepare for academic and career opportunities.

03

Amplifying voices, advocating for change

Many students go on to use their English to amplify their voices and advocate for change on the global stage.
